# InternVL: Scaling up Vision Foundation Models and Aligning for Generic Visual-Linguistic Tasks

**Authors:** Zhe Chen, Jiannan Wu, Wenhai Wang, Weijie Su, Guo Chen, Sen Xing, Muyan Zhong, Qinglong Zhang, Xizhou Zhu, Lewei Lu, Bin Li, Ping Luo, Tong Lu, Yu Qiao, Jifeng Dai

## Summary

InternVL is a large-scale vision-language foundation model that scales the vision encoder to 6 billion parameters (InternViT-6B) and progressively aligns it with a large language model through a language middleware called QLLaMA, an 8-billion-parameter multilingual extension of LLaMA. The training follows a three-stage strategy: (1) vision-language contrastive training on web-scale noisy image-text pairs, (2) vision-language generative training on filtered high-quality data with BLIP-2-style losses, and (3) supervised fine-tuning on 4M instruction samples with Vicuna-13B to create the multi-modal dialogue system InternVL-Chat. Unlike prior VLLMs that connect a roughly 1-billion-parameter vision encoder to the LLM with lightweight glue layers, InternVL balances the parameter scales of the two sides and lets the vision encoder serve both as a standalone visual backbone and as the visual component of a dialogue system. The paper reports state-of-the-art performance on 32 generic visual-linguistic benchmarks spanning image classification, semantic segmentation, zero-shot video classification, image-text retrieval, image captioning, and multi-modal dialogue. Because of its strong open-vocabulary recognition ability, InternVL is frequently used in HOI reconstruction pipelines as the visual front end that identifies which object the hand is manipulating before a matching 3D asset is retrieved.

## Background and Problem

The rapid growth of large language models has accelerated multimodal AGI systems, but the authors observe that vision and vision-language foundation models have not kept pace. Existing VLLMs commonly employ lightweight glue layers such as Q-Former or linear projection to align vision and language features, which the paper identifies as having three limitations: (1) disparity in parameter scale — the largest LLMs reach hundreds of billions of parameters while the widely used vision encoders remain around one billion, potentially under-using the LLM's capacity; (2) inconsistent representation — vision models trained on pure-vision data or aligned with BERT-style encoders produce features that connect poorly to LLMs; and (3) inefficient connection — Q-Former and linear-projection glue layers are lightweight and randomly initialized, failing to capture the rich cross-modal interactions needed for understanding and generation.

Scaling up the vision encoder is the natural remedy, but training such a model is hard: internet-scale image-text data is heterogeneous in quality and highly noisy, which challenges the training process, and generative supervision requires higher-quality data whose suitability for the vision side is an open question, along with the question of how to represent users' commands and the representations between the vision encoder and the LLM. Prior vision encoders derive features mainly from visual-only datasets like ImageNet or JFT, and the prevalent models connected to LLMs remain limited to around 1 billion parameters, which constrains VLLM performance.

## Method

The architecture has two components. The vision encoder InternViT-6B is a vanilla ViT scaled to 6 billion parameters (width 3200, depth 48, MLP 12800, 25 heads, 5903M parameters), selected through a hyperparameter search over depth {32, 48, 64, 80}, head dimension {64, 128}, and MLP ratio {4, 8} — 16 distinct models evaluated on a 100M subset of LAION-en for speed, accuracy, and stability, with throughput measured at 224x224 on a single A100. The language middleware QLLaMA is built from the pre-trained multilingual LLaMA by adding 96 learnable queries and cross-attention layers (about 1 billion randomly initialized parameters), enabling it to smoothly integrate visual elements into the language representation. Compared with a Q-Former, QLLaMA is initialized from pre-trained weights, is 42 times larger, and can serve both as a bridge between vision encoder and LLM decoder and as a standalone contrastive text encoder.

The "Swiss Army Knife" design offers flexible compositions: for visual perception tasks InternViT-6B is used alone (global average pooling plus linear projection for classification, feature maps for dense tasks); for contrastive tasks the vision features are pooled with attention pooling and scored against text encoded by QLLaMA's [EOS] token (InternVL-C), or the full InternVL model encodes both image and text (InternVL-G); for generative tasks QLLaMA's queries re-organize the visual representations of InternViT-6B as prefix texts for sequential token generation.

Training proceeds in three progressive stages. Stage 1 performs contrastive learning with a CLIP-style symmetric cross-entropy loss, using InternViT-6B as visual encoder and LLaMA-7B as text encoder, on web-scale noisy pairs from LAION-en, LAION-multi, LAION-COCO, COYO, CC12M, CC3M, Wukong, LAION-multilang, and TikTok — 6.03 billion image-text pairs originally, 4.98 billion after filtering extremely low-quality data. Stage 2 connects InternViT-6B with QLLaMA, keeping both frozen and training only the new queries and cross-attention layers, with a BLIP-2-style combination of image-text contrastive (ITC), image-text matching (ITM), and image-grounded text generation (ITG) losses on low-quality-filtered data reduced further to 1.03 billion samples; this stage enables zero-shot image captioning and improves retrieval. Stage 3 performs supervised fine-tuning, connecting InternViT-6B and QLLaMA to Vicuna-13B through MLP layers and training the MLPs (optionally the LLM layers) on approximately 4M high-quality instruction samples covering captioning, VQA, OCR, grounding, and conversation; training only the MLP layer (or plus the LLM's MLP) preserves the LLM's original language capabilities while accelerating SFT.

## Contributions

1. InternVL, the first large-scale vision-language foundation model to scale up the vision encoder to 6 billion parameters and align it with an LLM through the QLLaMA language middleware, achieving strong performance across visual perception, vision-language, and multi-modal dialogue tasks.
2. A progressive image-text alignment strategy for efficient training of large-scale vision-language models, which maximizes utilization of web-scale noisy data for contrastive learning and fine-grained high-quality data for generative learning.
3. The "Swiss Army Knife" model design in which the aligned InternViT-6B and QLLaMA can be flexibly composed for contrastive, generative, perception, and dialogue tasks rather than serving a single connection scheme.
4. Extensive evaluation against state-of-the-art vision foundation models and VLLMs on 32 benchmarks, showing that InternViT-6B is a viable strong alternative to ViT-22B at one quarter the parameter count.
5. Release of the model family (InternVL-C, InternVL-G, InternVL-Chat) that bridges the gap between scaled vision encoders and LLMs for the broader community.

## Experimental Setup

The model is trained on public data: Stage 1 uses 6.03B web-scale image-text pairs (LAION-en, LAION-multi, LAION-COCO, COYO, CC12M, CC3M, Wukong, LAION-multilang, TikTok), reduced to 4.98B after low-quality filtering; Stage 2 filters to 1.03B higher-quality pairs; Stage 3 collects about 4M instruction samples from COYO-Caption, TextCaps, VQAv2, OK-VQA, A-OKVQA, IconQA, OCR-VQA, ChartQA, DocVQA, UReader, ST-VQA, CLEVR, RefCOCOg/+/re, TinyHTML, LLaVA-150K, LRV-Instruction, LLaVA-Mix-665K, and other sources (training splits only). Ablations over InternViT-6B variants are conducted with contrastive learning on a 100M LAION-en subset over 10K iterations. Evaluation covers linear probing on ImageNet-1K plus seven variants (IN-ReaL, V2, A, R, Sketch, ObjectNet) and JFT-3B; semantic segmentation on ADE20K under linear probing, head tuning (UperNet), and full fine-tuning; zero-shot image classification on 20 datasets; multilingual ImageNet-1K in 5 languages and multilingual retrieval on XTD in 8 languages; zero-shot video classification on Kinetics-400/600/700; zero-shot image-text retrieval on Flickr30K and COCO (English and Chinese versions); zero-shot video-text retrieval on MSR-VTT; zero-shot and fine-tuned image captioning on COCO Karpathy, Flickr30K, and NoCaps; VQA on VQAv2, GQA, VizWiz, and TextVQA; and multi-modal dialogue on MME (14 subtasks) and POPE object-hallucination evaluation. Baselines include OpenCLIP, EVA-01/02-CLIP, MAWS-ViT-58B, ViT-22B, BLIP-2, InstructBLIP, and Qwen-VL, restricted to models trained on public data for fairness.

## Results

- Linear-probe image classification: InternViT-6B reaches 83.2% top-1 on ImageNet-1K, a very significant improvement over prior state-of-the-art without JFT data (ViT-22B 79.8%, EVA-01-CLIP-g 76.0%); MAWS-ViT-58B reports 87.8% but with the private JFT-3B dataset.
- Semantic segmentation on ADE20K: linear probing 47.2 mIoU versus 39.3 for ViT-22B (+7.9); head tuning with UperNet 54.9 versus 53.9; full fine-tuning 58.9 versus 53.9.
- Zero-shot image classification: 83.2 IN-1K, 85.8 IN-A, 95.7 IN-V2, 77.3 IN-R, 79.9 IN-Sketch, 80.6 ObjectNet, averaging 82.4 across benchmarks — surpassing EVA-02-CLIP-E+ by 0.8 average points and with the smallest gap between IN-1K and variant accuracy (0.8), indicating robustness to distribution shift; on 20 datasets the average is 78.1%, 1.0 above EVA-02-CLIP-E+.
- Multilingual ImageNet-1K (EN/ZH/JP/AR/IT): 83.2/64.5/61.5/44.9/65.7, averaging 64.0; multilingual XTD retrieval averages 96.6% recall@10 (InternVL-C 94.6, InternVL-G 96.6).
- Zero-shot video classification (8 frames, top-1/top-5): K400 65.9/76.1, K600 65.5/75.5, K700 56.8/66.5, exceeding EVA-02-CLIP-E+ by +6.3/+6.2/+4.1 top-1.
- Zero-shot image-text retrieval (English Flickr30K/COCO 1K test): InternVL-G reaches 97.9 R@1 image-to-text on Flickr30K and 89.3/93.2 (R@1/R@5) COCO image-to-text, averaging 88.3 across all metrics; on Chinese Flick30K-CN/COCO-CN InternVL-G averages 90.9.
- Zero-shot image captioning: QLLaMA alone scores 128.2 CIDEr on COCO Karpathy and 113.7 on NoCaps; after SFT, InternVL-Chat reaches 146.7 COCO, 92.2 Flickr30K, and 126.2 NoCaps.
- Multi-modal dialogue: InternVL-Chat achieves 1586.4 on MME and 87.6 on POPE; VQA scores of 66.6 (VQAv2), 58.8 (GQA), 58.5 (VizWiz), and 61.5 (TextVQA).
- Ablations show QLLaMA as glue yields large dialogue gains over an MLP layer under the same trainable-parameter budget (e.g., MME 1122.5 versus 970.5 with frozen Vicuna-7B, 1212.7 versus 983.3 with frozen Vicuna-13B), confirming that InternViT-6B's feature space is more consistent with off-the-shelf LLMs.

## Limitations

The training data remains a mixture of noisy web-scale pairs and curated instruction data, and the paper does not report an end-to-end evaluation under fully closed-loop dialogue with task planning. Stage-3 fine-tuning observes training annotations of some VQA datasets (VQAv2, GQA, VizWiz, TextVQA), so those dialogue/VQA comparisons are not strictly zero-shot. The vision encoder is trained and evaluated primarily on natural images (ImageNet, web photos, video frames), so its transfer to specialized domains such as medical imagery, remote sensing, or dense small-object scenes is only demonstrated on ADE20K-style segmentation rather than exhaustively validated. The 6-billion-parameter encoder and QLLaMA middleware impose substantial memory and compute requirements (the hyperparameter search shows throughput varying by roughly 3x across InternViT-6B variants at 224x224), which can be a burden for downstream deployments. The paper also notes that its dialogue configuration and additional ablation details are deferred to supplementary materials, and performance depends on the choice of attached LLM decoder (Vicuna-7B/13B), which itself limits the achievable dialogue quality.
