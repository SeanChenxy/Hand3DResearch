# HO-3D v3 (2021)

> Hampali, Deb Sarkar, Lepetit. *HO-3D v3: Improving the Accuracy of Hand-Object Annotations of the HO-3D Dataset.* arXiv 2107.00887, 2021. Zotero Key: `8UAIYS68`.

## Summary
HO-3D v3 is an algorithmic upgrade of the original HOnnotate annotation (v2): by refining the multi-view RGB-D joint optimization (more accurate contact / depth / silhouette losses, iterative refinement), it improves the overall accuracy of hand mesh and 6D object pose ground truth in HO-3D by about 34% and extends the dataset to 103,462 frames — the most commonly used training/evaluation data for current 3D hand-object reconstruction methods.

## 1. Dataset Purpose
- Solves the precision problem of HO-3D v2 (still has a 12.31 mm fingertip mean error) by improving HOnnotate's optimization objective.
- Tasks: 3D hand pose & shape, 6D object pose, joint hand-object reconstruction, contact estimation (the contact map in v3 is significantly more accurate).
- Same subjects and objects as v2; focused on single hand + rigid object.
- Also exemplifies an underestimated paradigm in the HOI community: "continuous maintenance / dataset versioning".

## 2. Data Composition
- Source: the same multi-view RGB-D capture as v2, with no new subjects / objects.
- Viewpoint: multi-view Intel RealSense RGB-D, tabletop setting.
- Scale: 83,325 training frames + 20,137 test frames = 103,462 frames (v2 has 77,558 frames).
- Object and action: 10 YCB objects, single-hand grasp/manipulation sequences, same action set as v2.
- The most obvious v3 improvement is the contact map: v3 annotations have denser contact coverage on the hand surface and lower mean penetration distance.

## 3. Annotation and Supervision
- Hand: 3D 21 joints, MANO β / θ, hand mesh (same format as v2, but more accurate values).
- Object: 6D pose (YCB-Video CAD).
- Contact: the main improvement of v3 — refined hand-object distance field, sub-pixel silhouette, and multi-frame contact consistency give a more accurate contact map and penetration metric.
- Camera / depth: same as v2, all camera intrinsics, extrinsics, and depth streams are kept.
- No robot annotations, no language.

## 4. Supported Evaluation
- Benchmark tasks identical to v2 (hand pose, object pose, joint reconstruction), but all metrics are based on the new, more accurate ground truth.
- Key metric: fingertip mean error (v2 = 12.31 mm, v3 = 8.12 mm, based on 50 manually re-annotated frames).
- In-paper ablation: each of the three algorithmic improvements (stronger silhouette fitting / temporal contact consistency / geometric refinement) is ablated separately, showing that each one further reduces the fingertip error.
- The evaluation server is separate from v2's, enabling the community to compare "method performance on the new ground truth".

## 5. Why It Matters
- Acts as the standard training + evaluation data for nearly all current 3D hand-object reconstruction methods (HMP, iHOI, AlignSDF, I2L-MeshNet-HOI, HOISDF, etc., all use v3).
- Promotes the "contact-aware" evaluation protocol on the HO-3D family.
- As a paradigm of "small-upgrade datasets", it shows that "continuously refining existing annotations" drives the field more than "releasing a new dataset".
- Provides reliable contact ground truth, giving contact-prior, grasp-affordance, and physics-based hand-object optimization directions a true basis.

## 6. Limitations and Biases
- Still 10 objects / 10 subjects: the scale limit remains.
- Still a tabletop / lab environment, limiting generalization to in-the-wild egocentric video.
- Improvements are validated against 50 manually re-annotated frames — small sample, statistically noisy.
- No articulated objects, no bi-manual, no dynamic contact (sliding / rolling), no tactile annotation.
- Ground-truth noise: MANO's bias on unnatural hand shapes means the v3 contact map can still have 1–2 mm error at fingertips.

## 7. Takeaway
HO-3D v3 is the de facto default benchmark for 3D hand-object reconstruction. It is best for demonstrating "the accuracy of joint 3D hand + 6D object + contact-map recovery on a more accurate ground truth". **Not suitable** for evaluating cross-object generalization (only 10 objects), bi-manual, articulated, or in-the-wild. In this survey, v3 plays the role of the "gold standard for 3D HOI reconstruction evaluation" and serves as a unified anchor for the SOTA comparison of all "HO-3D-evaluating" methods in Ch3 / Ch5 / Ch6.
