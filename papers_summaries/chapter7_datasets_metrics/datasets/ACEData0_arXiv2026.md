## Summary
Embodied datasets fragment the perception-action loop across viewpoints, modalities, and spatial scales: egocentric corpora lack ground-truth body/object motion, mocap HOI datasets omit the egocentric view and rarely record audio or tactile, and laboratory capture removes the occlusions and clutter of real homes. The paper introduces the Ambient Capture Engine (ACE), a system that turns real home environments into synchronized recording studios at two scales (table-scale for dexterous manipulation, room-scale for whole-body activity), and uses it to build ACE-Data-0: 150 hours, 17M frames, 75,000 interaction episodes, 200 task categories, 50 participants, and 50+ instrumented objects across 2 home sites, with egocentric and 8-view exocentric video, 60 Hz body/hand/object motion, multi-channel audio, and full-palm tactile pressure, all registered on one spatio-temporal frame. A three-level benchmark (tactile-from-vision, human motion estimation, hand-motion reconstruction from ego and exo views) evaluates 30+ methods against measured ground truth, exposing large gaps under contact, occlusion, egomotion, and long horizons.

## Background and Motivation
The paper argues existing resources fail in three ways. (1) Fragmented modalities: large egocentric datasets (Ego4D, Ego-Exo4D, EPIC-KITCHENS, Xperience-10M) provide no measured body/object state or synchronized third-person view, while mocap datasets (GRAB, ARCTIC, BEHAVE, OakInk2, HOT3D) omit egocentric video and almost all lack audio or tactile. (2) Unnatural environments: physically annotated corpora are captured in laboratories that eliminate furniture occlusion, spatial constraints, and object diversity. (3) Short horizons: most HOI clips span seconds and depict one movement, whereas real household activity is goal-directed, unfolds over minutes, and chains sub-tasks across the scene. ACE addresses all three simultaneously by instrumenting lived-in homes and prescribing goal-level instructions so that planning, hesitation, and behavioral variation enter the data naturally.

## Dataset Construction
ACE deploys two complementary configurations across two sites. The table-scale site is a 30-square-meter workspace with 25+ interactable objects from 8+ categories, covered by 8 close-range GoPro cameras (1920x1080 at 30 FPS, 0.3-0.5 m) and 16 OptiTrack PrimeX 22 cameras. The room-scale site is a fully furnished ~200-square-meter apartment (kitchen, dining, living room, bedroom) with 8 truss-mounted ZED One cameras and 12 OptiTrack cameras, keeping any point visible from at least 4 views. Participants wear a mocap suit with 41 markers, the ACE-Ego-Head-V02 Lite headset (four fisheye cameras, 1088x1280 at 20 FPS, IMU, five tracking markers), and Manus pose gloves plus ACE-Sense-Glove tactile gloves (full-palm pressure maps); every interactable object is 3D-scanned or 2D-Gaussian-Splatting reconstructed and fitted with at least four OptiTrack markers for 60 Hz 6-DoF trajectories. All streams align to the OptiTrack clock via a QR-code optical-clock procedure, reaching millisecond residuals (per-camera re-estimated offsets under 4 ms); camera calibration yields median reprojection errors below 3 px (exocentric) and about 2 px (egocentric). Tasks are recorded as three types: atomic HOI takes (~3 minutes; 6.2M+ frames, 36.5% of frames, 30,000+ episodes), chains of HOI (~20-30 minutes interleaved activities ending with the scene tidied; 7.3M+ frames, 42.9%, 40,000+ episodes), and human-scene interaction takes (~5 minutes of locomotion, exercise, sitting, lying; 3.5M+ frames, 20.6%). One hour of recording produces roughly 1 TB of raw data. Annotations, mostly measured rather than estimated, comprise per-frame object category, 2D/3D bounding boxes, 6-DoF poses, meshes, and motion trails; 41-joint body skeletons, articulated hand poses (42 hand joints) with SMPL-X parameters, and pixel-aligned projections onto every view; tactile contact labels; multi-channel audio; and Gemini-generated, human-checked natural-language captions.

## Evaluation Protocol
The benchmark holds out 10 hours of capture as a test set and evaluates released pre-trained checkpoints across three hierarchical tracks. (1) Tactile from vision: predict full-hand grasp pressure from egocentric video, scored by temporal accuracy, contact IoU, volumetric IoU, and center-of-pressure error, with PressureVision, EgoPressureDiff, and TouchAnything as baselines. (2) Human motion estimation: recover articulated body pose and world trajectory from multi-view, single-view exocentric, or egocentric input, scored by PA-MPJPE, PA-PVE, MPJPE, PVE, and WA-MPJPE against mocap ground truth; 22 methods are evaluated, mostly on room-scale recordings. (3) Hand motion estimation: predict both hands as MANO sequences from egocentric or exocentric video, scored by PA-MPJPE, MPJPE, F@5, F@15, AUCJ, and world-frame trajectory error; ground truth comes from Manus gloves (room-scale) or RANSAC triangulation plus manual refinement (table-scale), with unreliable joints masked. Because ego and exo views observe the same takes, the protocol supports a direct cross-viewpoint comparison. No robot policy training is evaluated; the dataset is positioned as a training substrate for imitation learning, world models, and VLA systems.

## Findings and Analysis
Tactile-from-vision shows a substantial generalization gap: the best method, TouchAnything, reaches only 0.7095 temporal accuracy, 0.1646 C-IoU, 0.1357 V-IoU, and 6.5846 CoP on the table-scale test set, versus 0.0093/0.0007 for PressureVision, indicating that detecting when contact occurs is far easier than recovering where pressure is distributed under hand-object occlusion. In human motion estimation, strong local pose accuracy coexists with poor global trajectories: the best PA-MPJPE is 55.7 mm (SMPLest-X, single-view temporal), while the best world-frame WA-MPJPE is 180.2 mm (Human3R, scene-aware); scene-aware methods mainly improve trajectory rather than joint configuration, and egocentric methods lag badly (EgoEgo: 159.6 mm PA-MPJPE). For egocentric hand motion, per-frame WildHands achieves 11.2 mm PA-MPJPE but world-space methods Dyn-HaMR and HaWoR show trajectory errors of 98.2 mm and 102.1 mm; in contrast, exocentric regressors reach 9.1-10.8 mm PA-MPJPE (best: WiLoR at 9.1 mm, F@5 0.313, AUCJ 0.819) and video-based HaPTIC attains a 63.0 mm trajectory error. The cross-view analysis concludes that egomotion estimation, not finger articulation, is the dominant error source in egocentric reconstruction, and that ego and exo views fail in complementary ways (truncation/distortion versus occlusion).

## Contributions
- ACE, a two-scale ambient capture methodology (table-scale and room-scale) that records synchronized egocentric video, 8-view exocentric video, 60 Hz body/hand/object motion, audio, and tactile signals in real homes, with millisecond-level synchronization and measured camera poses.
- ACE-Data-0: 150+ hours, 17M+ frames, 75,000+ episodes, 200+ task categories, 50+ participants and 50+ objects, with long-horizon chains of household activities and goal-level instructions.
- Five annotation families largely derived from measured physical states (poses, projections, boxes, trails, contact) plus language captions.
- A three-level benchmark of 30+ state-of-the-art methods covering vision-to-touch prediction, full-body motion recovery, and egocentric/exocentric hand-motion reconstruction, with the reported claim of being the broadest human-motion-estimation evaluation in real home scenes to date.

## Limitations
The paper explicitly states that ACE covers only two sites, so layout, furnishing, and lighting variation are limited. Ground truth is restricted to instrumented entities: tracked objects must be scanned and marker-equipped in advance, and state changes of articulated mechanisms, fluids, and deformable materials are not annotated. The mocap suit, gloves, headset, and markers remain visible in the recordings and may introduce dataset-specific visual cues. The benchmark additionally leaves object pose estimation for future work (too few applicable methods) and does not include any robot-learning experiments; hand ground truth at table scale is triangulated and manually refined rather than directly measured, and textual captions are model-generated with human review.
