# FPHA — First-Person Hand Action (CVPR 2018)

> Garcia-Hernando, Yuan, Baek, Kim. *First-Person Hand Action Benchmark with RGB-D Videos and 3D Hand Pose Annotations.* CVPR 2018. DOI: 10.1109/CVPR.2018.00050. Zotero Key: `8NWKU8IN`.

## Summary
FPHA is the first first-person (egocentric) 3D hand pose + 6D object pose + RGB-D video + 45-class action hand-object interaction benchmark: 100K+ frames, 6-magnetic-sensor mocap, 26 objects, 6 subjects. It is the pioneering dataset of egocentric HOI.

## 1. Dataset Purpose
- Solves the lack of "first-person 3D hand pose + 6D object pose" synchronous ground truth. HO-3D and others focus on the third view, while FPHA focuses on the first view.
- Tasks: (1) egocentric 3D hand pose estimation; (2) egocentric 6D object pose estimation; (3) first-person action recognition (45 classes); (4) RGB-D + pose multi-modal action recognition.
- Mixed bi-manual / single-hand; focused on "first-person everyday operations between people and objects".
- A pioneer dataset of first-person HOI, long used as a reference for egocentric 3D hand pose evaluation.

## 2. Data Composition
- Source: real capture. 6 subjects operate 26 everyday objects under the first-person view in natural environments.
- Viewpoint: first-person RGB-D (Intel RealSense fixed on the subject's head / chest).
- Scale: 100,579 frames, 1,175 video sequences.
- Object and action: 26 everyday objects (soup spoon, seasoning bottle, remote control, notebook, mug, etc.); 45 actions (open lid, write, pour water, cut vegetables, etc.).
- Contains severe hand-object occlusion (the core challenge is that the subject cannot see his own hand under the egocentric view).
- No bi-manual specialized design, no articulated object joint tracking, no robot.

## 3. Annotation and Supervision
- Hand: 3D 21 joints, obtained automatically by 6 magnetic sensors mocap + inverse kinematics (no marker gloves required). MANO shape / pose fitting.
- Object: 6D pose (a subset provides 3D object model + 6D pose).
- Interaction: 45 action labels, object class labels.
- Scene: first-person RGB + depth (no external multi-view), camera intrinsics / extrinsics.
- No language, no tactile, no multi-view.

## 4. Supported Evaluation
- Benchmark tasks: (1) hand pose estimation (MPJPE / PA-MPJPE); (2) 6D object pose estimation (ADD / ADD-S / AUC); (3) RGB-D + pose action recognition (Top-1 / Top-5).
- Key metrics: MPJPE, PA-MPJPE, ADD, ADD-S, AUC-ADDS, action Top-1.
- Provides two splits: "seen subject" and "unseen subject".
- One of the standard evaluation protocols of first-person HOI (alongside H2O, Epic-Kitchens, etc.).

## 5. Why It Matters
- The first "first-person + 3D hand pose + 6D object pose" trinity dataset, establishing the egocentric HOI evaluation paradigm.
- The magnetic mocap + IK annotation pipeline does not depend on external optical systems, inspiring many subsequent large-scale egocentric annotation solutions after Ego4D.
- The 45 action labels provide an early benchmark for first-person action recognition.
- 18 baselines + SOTA were widely compared at the time, becoming a routine comparison source for hand pose / action recognition papers.
- Together with H2O and Epic-Kitchens, it forms the "early egocentric HOI trilogy".

## 6. Limitations and Biases
- Only 6 subjects: low style diversity.
- 26 objects + 45 actions: relatively small scale, methods easily overfit.
- Single RGB-D viewpoint: severe self-occlusion under the first-person view, and no external multi-view as a hard GT source.
- Magnetic sensor calibration accuracy is limited (millimeter level), not as accurate as optical mocap.
- No contact map, no affordance, no articulated object, no language.
- No bi-manual 6D pose joint ground truth (only single-hand 6D pose ground truth in a subset).
- After 2020, the dataset is gradually replaced by H2O / HOI4D / HOT3D as the main egocentric benchmark.

## 7. Takeaway
FPHA is best for demonstrating the capability of "first-person 3D hand pose + 6D object pose + action recognition", especially the usability of the magnetic mocap annotation pipeline. **Not suitable** for evaluating bi-manual, articulated, tactile, language-conditioned, or large-scale generalization. In this survey, FPHA plays the role of "early pioneering benchmark of egocentric HOI + historical reference of first-person hand pose" and is positioned as the "canonical first-person HOI source before the emergence of HO-3D v3, H2O, and HOT3D".
